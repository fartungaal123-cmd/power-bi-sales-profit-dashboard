# Sales & Profit Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance and profitability using the Financial Sample dataset.

The goal is to identify sales trends, compare customer segments, evaluate product performance, and communicate business insights through an interactive dashboard.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Data visualization
- KPI analysis
- Business performance analysis

## Key Performance Indicators
- **Total Sales:** 118.73 million
- **Total Profit:** 16.89 million
- **Profit Margin:** 14.23%

## Key Findings

### Sales Trend
Sales increased from approximately 26.4 million in 2013 to 92.3 million in 2014.

### Product Performance
Paseo generated the highest total sales and the highest total profit among the products in the dataset.

### Customer Segment Performance
The Government segment generated the highest sales among the customer segments.

### Profitability
Carretera generated the lowest total profit among the products. Further analysis is needed to understand the factors behind its performance.

## Business Recommendations
- Investigate the factors contributing to Paseo's strong performance.
- Examine Carretera's sales, costs, and profit margin.
- Analyze sales drivers within the Government segment.
- Investigate the increase in sales between 2013 and 2014.

## Project Status
The dashboard was created as a data analytics portfolio project to practice data visualization, KPI analysis, and business insight communication.
